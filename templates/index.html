        Scientific & Academic Validation

        Forensic Standards & Methodology

        1. ISO/IEC 27042:2015 — Analysis & Interpretation of Digital Evidence

        Mandates strict separation between objective facts (verified log records) and analytical hypotheses (inferred attacker behavior). CRIME404 enforces this separation across its timeline, classification matrix, and exported reports.

        2. NIST SP 800-86 & SHA-256 Chain of Custody

        Every uploaded artifact is hashed immediately upon ingestion using cryptographic SHA-256 (`hashlib.sha256`), preserving original binary integrity and UTC ingestion provenance.

        3. MITRE ATT&CK® Behavioral Mapping & Belief Revision

        Heuristic rules map multi-source events to standardized adversary techniques (e.g., T1110 Brute Force, T1078 Valid Accounts, T1059 Command Execution, T1041 Exfiltration) and dynamically recalculate hypothesis plausibility as new logs are added or removed.

        4. SWGDE / OSAC Error Mitigation & Gap Detection

        Rather than assuming continuity when logs are missing, CRIME404 explicitly flags temporal blind spots and cross-source session contradictions and prompts the analyst with targeted evidence acquisition queries.
